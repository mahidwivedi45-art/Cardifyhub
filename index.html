Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with CardifyHub.

How does CardifyHub handle data security?
CardifyHub uses Firebase Authentication for secure access control, ensuring your business workspace data is fully isolated to your account.
Can I use CardifyHub without paid integrations?
Yes! You can instantly import orders and inventory using CSV files or manual entry on the Free plan without any paid tool dependency.
What happens if I exceed my monthly order limit?
You will receive an automated notification suggesting an upgrade to our Growth ($12/mo) or Pro ($30/mo) plan to support higher order volumes.
